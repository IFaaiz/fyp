# First expansion tranche: classification quality gate

This 100-record tranche is an intermediate contribution to a larger AI-silver
dataset, not a training-ready dataset or an evaluation set. It was sampled from
the 8,000 Enron candidate pool before the cross-source leakage audit. The
source emails and both blind reviewer outputs remain in ignored `ai/data/`.

Two GPT-6 Luna xhigh reviewers independently read the same subject and current
message using the existing v3 rules. They produced classification labels,
review flags, and short notes; no extraction spans were collected. A supervisor
then read all 17 disagreements and all 27 records flagged by either reviewer
(some overlap), the sole exact unflagged project-positive agreement, a
deterministic 15-record sample of unflagged NON_PROJECT agreements, and 12
additional agreements chosen for difficult business/project boundaries.

| Gate result | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Reviewed source records | 100 |
| Exact label agreement | 83 |
| Any review flag | 27 |
| Exact agreement without review flag | 68 |
| Supervisor exclusions despite exact agreement | 2 |
| Accepted provisional AI silver | 66 |
| Excluded as uncertain | 34 |

Of the accepted rows, 65 are `NON_PROJECT`. One project-positive record about
an explicitly named software upgrade and user transition has `DEADLINE`,
`ACTION_REQUEST`, and `GENERAL_UPDATE`. The supervisor removed two apparent
`NON_PROJECT` agreements because their system access and customer onboarding
messages could plausibly belong to managed project work. This is evidence that
blind agreement does not establish correctness. The accepted negative examples
include personal messages, routine HR and corporate notices, trading operations,
business meetings without project scope, event dates that are not deliverable
deadlines, attached documents without document requests, and routine access
approval notices. No abstention or disagreement was promoted to silver.

The text-free, reproducible decisions are in
`ai/annotation/training_expansion_100_silver_decisions.jsonl`, generated by
`ai/scripts/adjudicate_training_expansion_100.py`. They remain **AI silver**;
none are human reviewed or gold. Leakage grouping still gates their use in a
train/validation split. The class imbalance in this tranche requires further
project-focused selection before model training.

A second independent quality pass inspected **all 66 accepted rows** and
identified five possible scope-boundary cases without an unambiguous missed
project label. The supervisor reread those five and retained the existing
decisions: their authored messages concern personal plans, external
sponsorship, routine trading, deal operations, and a benefits-processing
incident. The cases and IDs are recorded in
`ai/reports/training_expansion_100_second_audit.md`; they remain AI silver
judgments that require human calibration before any final claim.
